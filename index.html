ZORO
Zo Re-Unification Organisation
General Headquarters : Aizawl, Zoram

The Zo Re-Unification Organisation (ZORO) is dedicated to the historical reunification of the partitioned Zo ethnic communities spread across international borders, seeking to integrate them under a single, peaceful administration while safeguarding their collective identity and indigenous rights.
